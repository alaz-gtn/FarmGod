# 1.1.0

- **Crafting cost in item tooltips**: for any item a profession makes, even if none of your characters has that profession. You get what its materials cost with your Auctionator or TSM prices (each at the cheaper of the auction house and a vendor that sells it), and how much cheaper or dearer that is than buying it at the auction house, in green or red. You also see the profession and skill it takes, and the cost per unit when the recipe makes several. Hold Shift to see each material. A material without a price shows "no price" instead of a wrong total. The recipes come with FarmGod, from AtlasLoot Forever's data, including the recipes Forever added. No recipe addon needed. You can turn it off in the Prices tab.
- The session no longer pauses by itself after 10 minutes with nothing looted, so your gold per hour counts the real time. It's an option now (Session tab), off by default.
